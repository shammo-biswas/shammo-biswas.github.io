A little about me

I’m a researcher working at the intersection of vision, language, and learning.

I’m a Research Assistant at the Computer Vision and Intelligence (CVIS) Lab at BRAC University, under the supervision of Dr. Md. Ashraful Alam. I’m also pursuing a master’s in Computer Science and Engineering.

My work spans computer vision, 3D vision, and vision-language models, with interests in satellite imagery, biomedical imaging, and natural language processing. I’m interested in how models learn useful representations and connect visual information with language.
